Tile Rules

  • Rockford (Player): Placed at starting coordinates (X, Y). Rockford must spawn in an empty tile.
  • Steel Wall (Tile 3): Indestructible boundary. Surround cave perimeter with steel walls.
  • Exit Door (Tile 4): Looks like a standard wall until the diamond quota is reached, then transforms into a Portal.
  • Brick Wall (Tile 5): Standard destructible wall; boulders and diamonds roll off its edges.
  • Magic Wall (Tile 6): Looks like a wall until struck by a falling boulder, then converts boulders into diamonds.
  • Firefly (Tile 11) & Butterfly (Tile 12): Patrol along empty spaces. Crushing a Firefly causes a 3×3 explosion; crushing a Butterfly yields 9 diamonds!
  • Amoeba (Tile 13): Expands slowly; turns into diamonds if trapped, or petrifies into Tile 15 if overgrown.
